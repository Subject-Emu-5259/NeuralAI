Training

Forged through preference learning.

NeuralAI is aligned with Direct Preference Optimization. Dataset v15 expanded to 597 preference pairs focused on debugging, logic, and multi-step reasoning — trained 3 epochs / 450 steps (loss 0.305, margin ~3.5) on Apple Silicon MPS, adapter live on HuggingFace Subject-Emu-5259/NeuralAI.
