# History of Music & Sound

## 🎼 Classical Era
- **Baroque (1600-1750)**: Bach, Handel, Vivaldi; birth of the opera and concerto.
- **Classical (1750-1820)**: Mozart, Haydn, Beethoven; emphasis on clarity, balance, and the sonata form.
- **Romantic (1820-1900)**: Chopin, Wagner, Tchaikovsky; emotional intensity and nationalistic themes.

## 🎷 Jazz & Blues
- **Origins**: African American communities in the Southern US, early 20th century.
- **Evolution**: Swing, Bebop, Cool Jazz, and Fusion. Key figures: Miles Davis, Coltrane.

## 🎸 The Rock Revolution
- **1950s**: Rock and Roll (Elvis, Chuck Berry).
- **1960s**: The British Invasion (Beatles) and Psychedelic Rock.
- **1970s**: Punk, Heavy Metal, and Progressive Rock.

## 🎧 Electronic & Hip-Hop
- **Hip-Hop**: Bronx, NY (1970s); Turntablism, MCing, and cultural storytelling.
- **Electronic**: Synthesizer development (Moog) to modern EDM and Ambient.

## 🎻 Global Traditions
- **Indian Classical**: Raga and Tala systems.
- **West African Drumming**: Polyrhythmic foundations for modern Western rhythm.
