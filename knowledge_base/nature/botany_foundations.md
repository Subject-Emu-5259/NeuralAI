# 🌿 Botany & Plant Biology (v1.0)

## Overview
The scientific study of plants, including their structure, properties, and biochemical processes.

## Core Concepts
- **Photosynthesis**: The process by which plants convert light into energy.
- **Plant Anatomy**: Roots, stems, leaves, and reproductive organs.
- **Taxonomy**: The classification of plants into families and species.
- **Ecology**: The role of plants in global ecosystems and the carbon cycle.

## Future Research
Deep dive into medicinal plants and agricultural optimization for sustainable survival.
