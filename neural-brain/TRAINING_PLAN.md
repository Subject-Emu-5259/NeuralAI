# 🧠 NeuralAI World-Brain Training Plan

This document outlines the structured acquisition of general-world knowledge to expand the intelligence and cultural context of the NeuralAI ecosystem. Since base model weights are static, this "training" is implemented as a high-density semantic knowledge base used for RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and context-injection.

## 🎯 Objective
To transform NeuralAI from a technical engineering engine into a universal intelligence capable of deep reasoning across all facets of human and natural existence.

## 📚 Knowledge Domains & Syllabus

### 🌿 Nature & Life (Directory: `/nature`)
- **Plants**: Botany, photosynthesis, medicinal plants, forest ecology, agriculture.
- **Animals**: Zoology, evolutionary biology, animal behavior, marine life, avian studies.
- **Creatures**: Cryptids, mythological creatures, extinct species, deep-sea anomalies.
- **The World**: Geology, meteorology, astronomy, planetary science, environmental systems.

### 📜 Humanity & History (Directory: `/history`)
- **World History**: Ancient civilizations, the Middle Ages, Industrial Revolution, Modern Era.
- **Humanity**: Anthropology, evolutionary history of Homo sapiens, societal evolution.
- **Religions & Beliefs**: Major world religions, indigenous spiritualities, philosophy, theology, mysticism.
- **Sociology**: Human behavior, political structures, economic history, cultural shifts.

### 🎨 Arts & Expression (Directory: `/arts`)
- **Music**: Theory, genres, historical composers, the physics of sound, modern production.
- **Movies & Cinema**: Film history, directing techniques, screenwriting, cinematic movements.
- **Fine Arts**: Painting, sculpture, architecture, digital art, art history.
- **Literature**: Classical texts, poetry, narrative structures, linguistic evolution.

## 🛠️ Implementation Strategy

1.  **Deep Research Cycles**: Use the `web_research` tool to gather high-density data on specific sub-topics.
2.  **Semantic Documentation**: Write structured `.md` files with clear headings, cross-references, and "Key Insights" sections.
3.  **Integration**: Feed these documents into the NeuralAI context window during relevant queries to simulate "training."
4.  **Verification**: Cross-reference facts across multiple high-authority sources to ensure accuracy.
