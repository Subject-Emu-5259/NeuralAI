# 🗺️ Modern Global Geopolitics: The Multipolar Order (2025-2026)

This asset analyzes the shifting power dynamics of the current international system.

## 🌍 The Multipolar Shift
The world has transitioned from a unipolar (US-led) order to a complex, multipolar system.

### 1. The Strategic Axis (US vs. China)
- **The Central Rivalry**: The dominant axis of global competition, spanning technology (AI/Chips), economy, and military posture.
- **Geoeconomic Weapons**: The use of export controls on critical materials and tariffs as strategic leverage.

### 2. Energy Security & Chokepoints
- **Critical Chokepoints**: The Strait of Hormuz as a linchpin for global oil supply; disruptions here cause immediate global shocks.
- **Energy Transition**: The race for critical minerals (Lithium, Cobalt) replacing oil as the primary geopolitical lever.

### 3. The Rise of Middle Powers
- **Agency of the Global South**: India, Gulf states, and Southeast Asia now act as "swing states," leveraging their position between the US and China to extract concessions.
