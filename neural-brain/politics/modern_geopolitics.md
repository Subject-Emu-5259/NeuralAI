# 🌍 Modern Geopolitics: The Global Power Shift

## 🏗️ The Multi-Polar World
- **Decline of Unipolarity**: Transition from a US-led unipolar world to a multi-polar system involving China, the EU, India, and regional powers.
- **Technological Sovereignty**: Control over AI, semiconductors, and energy transitions as the new frontier of global power.
- **The Belt and Road Initiative**: China's global infrastructure strategy to connect Asia with Africa and Europe.

## 🛡️ Strategic Blocs & Alliances
- **NATO**: Ongoing expansion and pivoting toward the Indo-Pacific.
- **BRICS+**: Expansion of the economic bloc as a counter-weight to Western financial systems.
- **ASEAN**: Maintaining neutrality in the US-China competition.

## ⚡ The AI Geopolitical Frontier
The development of AGI is the "Manhattan Project" of the 21st century. NeuralAI and NeuralLabs aim to provide an independent, decentralized intelligence layer that transcends borders.
