# 🌌 Quantum Physics: The Fabric of Reality

## 🏗️ Core Principles
- **Wave-Particle Duality**: Every particle or quantum entity may be described as either a particle or a wave.
- **Quantum Superposition**: A fundamental principle of quantum mechanics that states that, much like waves in classical physics, any two (or more) quantum states can be added together ("superposed") and the result will be another valid quantum state.
- **Quantum Entanglement**: A physical phenomenon that occurs when a group of particles are generated, interact, or share spatial proximity in a way such that the quantum state of each particle of the group cannot be described independently of the state of the others.
- **Uncertainty Principle**: Proposed by Werner Heisenberg, it states that the more precisely the position of some particle is determined, the less precisely its momentum can be known from initial conditions, and vice versa.

## 🔬 Significant Milestones
1. **1900**: Max Planck proposes that energy is radiated in discrete "quanta."
2. **1905**: Albert Einstein explains the photoelectric effect using quanta.
3. **1924**: Louis de Broglie proposes that matter has wave-like properties.
4. **1927**: The Solvay Conference marks the formalization of modern quantum theory.

## 🚀 Implications for NeuralAI
Quantum computing will eventually revolutionize the speed and efficiency of neural network training and inference. Understanding these principles allows for future-proofing architectures for "Neural-Quantum" transitions.
